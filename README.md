# Bridge (渡) — Palliative Care Navigation Platform

A bilingual (EN/中文) WeChat Mini Program prototype for end-of-life communication and palliative care navigation in China.

## Project Overview

Built at the [2026 Tsinghua International Summer School](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/i6DeBp2ufwmxH5vpGkk8hA) — *Culture and Mental Health: Tradition, Science, and Global Dialogue* — held August 10–23, 2026 in Beijing, hosted by the Department of Psychological and Cognitive Sciences and organized by the Research Center for Culture and Psychology, Tsinghua University. I was one of 20 participants selected from 13 countries.

Of the four team projects presented at the closing ceremony, *Bridging the Silence* received the **Director's Choice award** from Prof. Chao-Gan Yan, Director of the Research Center for Culture and Psychology, selected on problem significance, cultural appropriateness, and the prototype's potential for further development.

Inspired by Prof. Jun Jing's lecture *Bringing Death Back into Life*, the project addresses a gap in public awareness of palliative care in China: national pilot programs now cover 185 cities and districts, yet in one survey only about two in five respondents could explain what hospice and palliative care is.

We deliver it as a WeChat Mini Program because it opens directly from a link or QR code shared in a family group chat, requires no installation, and runs on inexpensive devices.
